01

Introduction

ELARA is committed to respecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information may be collected through our website, enquiry forms, account features, and interactions with our clinic.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where applicable, we will obtain your consent before processing information that requires it under relevant law.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details: name, email address, and phone number submitted through forms.
  • Account information: details required to create and manage an account, where account features are enabled.
  • Enquiry information: messages, questions, and appointment requests you choose to submit.
  • Technical information: browser type, device information, and basic website usage data where collected.
  • Medical information: relevant skin concerns or medical details that you voluntarily provide through an appropriate clinic process.

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive medical information through a general website contact form.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Coordinate appointment-related communication.
  • Provide and maintain account features.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Protect our website against misuse and security threats.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We will not use your information for a purpose that requires additional consent without obtaining that consent where legally required.

04

Medical Information

Information about your skin concerns or treatment history may be sensitive. If you provide medical information directly to our clinic, we will handle it according to applicable privacy, confidentiality, and healthcare requirements.

Website enquiries are not a substitute for a medical consultation. Do not use a general enquiry form for emergencies or urgent medical concerns.

05

When We Share Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers that support website hosting, communication, or technical operations.
  • Authorised personnel involved in responding to your request or providing relevant services.
  • Public authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Where appropriate, we seek to ensure that service providers handle information securely and only for authorised purposes.

06

Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use caution when submitting information online and keep your account credentials private.

07

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to requests, maintaining relevant records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be entitled to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a concern about how your information is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

09

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, and understand website performance where such tools are implemented.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

10

Children's Privacy

Our website is not designed to encourage children to submit personal information independently. Where information about a child is needed for clinic services, it should be provided by an authorised parent or guardian in accordance with applicable law.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices, website features, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Please review this page periodically for relevant updates.

12

Contact Us

If you have a question or concern about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, contact the ELARA team.
