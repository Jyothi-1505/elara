GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before your visit?

Here are a few things to know before discussing treatment options with our team.

Contact our team
How do I know which treatment is right for me?

A consultation helps a qualified professional understand your concerns and discuss suitable options. The right approach varies by individual.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Assessment may be needed before recommending a treatment. Contact the clinic to understand the consultation process for your concern.

Will I receive aftercare guidance?

Aftercare recommendations depend on the treatment and your individual needs. Ask your provider about care instructions and any follow-up visits.

Can I discuss more than one skin concern?

Yes. Share your concerns when arranging your consultation so the team can advise you about the appropriate assessment.
