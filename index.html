GOOD TO KNOW

Questions About Your Visit?

Find answers to common questions before your first consultation.

Ask Our Team
How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment link to access the patient portal. Follow the booking process to select an available appointment.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

A qualified clinician can assess your concerns and discuss suitable options, benefits, risks, and expected aftercare.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring relevant medical and skincare information, a list of medications, and any questions you would like to discuss.

Can I track my treatment progress?

The planned patient portal includes treatment history and progress-photo features. Availability depends on the portal implementation and your clinic's record-management process.
